The school-provided PhonePe QR image is installed here as `phonepe-payment-qr.jpg` and will display in the student payment step. Alternatively, set `NEXT_PUBLIC_PAYMENT_QR_IMAGE_URL` to a school-controlled image URL.

Do not replace the QR with a generated or unverified payment code. The app asks the student to enter a transaction reference, but school staff still need to verify payment before enabling the exam.
